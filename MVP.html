How SĀTHI works

One continuous loop, from goal to correction.

SĀTHI doesn't just play a tutorial video. It sets a plan, watches the attempt, measures it against biomechanically sound targets, and adjusts the next step — the same loop a human coach runs, automated.

◎
01
Set the goal
User states what to learn — a squat routine, a dance step, a chapter to revise.
▤
02
Build the plan
SĀTHI drafts a personalized session — targets, reps, sequence and pacing.
◉
03
Watch (camera + mic)
Live pose, joint and gesture tracking on-device via MediaPipe, no upload needed.
∠
04
Analyze motion
Posture, joint angles, trajectory, timing and reps measured against the target form.
%
05
Score & correct
0–100 score plus a specific spoken cue — "straighten your back", not just "wrong".
↻
06
Adapt next lesson
Progress is logged and the next session is retuned to what was actually weak.

Under the hood

Technical approach

The demo above runs a slice of this stack directly in your browser. The full system adds voice interaction, persistent progress tracking and cloud-scale plan generation.

App & backend
  • Flutter (Dart) client
  • FastAPI (Python) backend
  • PostgreSQL for persistent data
  • Redis for live session state
AI / ML
  • OpenCV + MediaPipe pose & gesture recognition
  • TensorFlow Lite for on-device inference
  • Multimodal LLM/API for reasoning & feedback
Real-time & voice
  • WebRTC / WebSocket streaming
  • Speech-to-text for spoken input
  • Text-to-speech for spoken coaching
Infra
  • Docker + CI/CD deployment
  • Cloud with optional edge AI
  • Low-latency, privacy-friendly inference
Why it matters

Coaching, without the cost of a coach.
